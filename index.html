    FAQ.

    Can I lose money?

    No. All the money is fake: no real order is placed and no account is connected.

    What's the difference between the AI Studio and Bullabo?

    The AI Studio is the core: free lessons, real market prices live with paper money, and your AI agents. Bullabo is a fast-forward simulation on fake prices, to practise fast and over the long run.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Open it and play. Your progress is saved on your device.

    I know nothing about trading. Is it for me?

    Yes. Bullabo has a beginner mode that explains every tool, and the AI Studio offers 63 lessons with animations and quizzes.

    Do my agents keep going when I close the app?

    Yes, in a way: when you come back, the Studio fetches the real prices from while you were away (up to 3 days) and has your agents replay them minute by minute, with the same engine as live. Then it shows you the summary.

    Is this investment advice?

    No. It's a learning tool. Simulated results don't predict future results, and an agent can lose all of its paper capital.
